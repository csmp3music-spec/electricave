# MBTA Rolling Stock Notes

Date: March 29, 2026

## Summary

- Red line visual: updated toward the MBTA 01400 "Bluebird" look, using the preserved Seashore cars as the historical paint reference.
- Orange line visual: updated toward the Hawker-Siddeley 01200 series / "Orange Blossoms" profile, with three side-door positions per car.
- Blue line visual: updated toward the compact East Boston profile with roof power gear, mixing cues from the 0600/0700 era.

## Source Notes

- Red Line Bluebirds:
  - Seashore Trolley Museum confirms Nos. 01450/01455 are MBTA 01400-series cars and notes restoration to the original blue, white, and gold colors.
  - Source: https://trolleymuseum.org/01400s/

- Orange Line 01200:
  - nycsubway.org roster states the Orange Line 01200 Hawker-Siddeley cars were nicknamed "Orange Blossoms" and had six doors, three on each side.
  - Source: https://www.nycsubway.org/wiki/MBTA_Orange_Line

- Blue Line 0600 / 0700:
  - nycsubway.org roster states the Blue Line used Hawker-Siddeley 0600 cars from 1979 to 2011 and Siemens 0700 cars beginning in 2007.
  - The same source notes the 0700 cars are stainless steel and have two pairs of doors per side.
  - Source: https://www.nycsubway.org/wiki/MBTA_Blue_Line

## Interpretation

- I did not find a reliable source using the "Bluebird" nickname for Blue Line cars.
- Based on the cited sources, "Bluebird" is documented for the Red Line 01400 fleet.
- The Blue Line scene therefore uses Blue Line-specific body cues rather than reusing the Red Line Bluebird paint treatment.
